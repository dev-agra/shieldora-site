About Us

Shieldora is a premium paint protection film brand by Prospera Global. We bring together world-class TPU from Lubrizol (USA) and Covestro (Germany) with Ashland and Henkel optically clear adhesives to give your vehicle's paint lasting protection.

From luxury cars to daily drivers, our Apex, Elite and Aura ranges deliver self-healing performance, brilliant gloss and clarity, backed by dependable post sales support.
